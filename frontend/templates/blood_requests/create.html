What happens after you submit?

Your request goes through the matching engine before notifications are sent.

Donor blood-group compatibility is checked.
Recent donation history is checked against the configured donation interval.
Donor distance from the hospital is calculated.
Eligible donors receive an in-app notification.
Donor contact information remains private until a donor accepts.
Need help?

Make sure the hospital coordinates are accurate. Distance is calculated from these coordinates, so incorrect values can affect matching.
